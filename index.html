Methods we trust

No marketing fads.
Just what the soil actually responds to.

Method 01

JADAM (Korean natural farming)

Ultra-low-cost microbial inputs you make yourself — JMS, JLF, JWA. Cho Youngsang's open-source farming system. Near-zero cost, built from local materials.

Method 02

Vermicompost ("black gold")

Worm-castings amendment. Peer-reviewed meta-analysis: +26% commercial yield, +57% root biomass. The strongest-evidenced biological input we sell.

Method 03

Johnson-Su bioreactor compost

Static, no-turn, fungal-dominant compost protocol developed by Dr. David Johnson. 12-month cycle. Applied as extract, slurry, or seed coating.

Method 04

Soil testing + interpretation

Lab chemistry (Logan / Ward / RegenAg) + biological health (Haney test, PLFA) + field reads (slake, infiltration, Solvita). Numbers turned into a 90-day plan.

Method 05

Food-forest + agroforestry design

Native species + non-invasive companions, climate-matched, hurricane-resilient. Tropical and temperate. No invasive recommendations — ever.

Method 06

Long-term stewardship

Soil work is multi-year. Retainer engagements track outcomes against baseline tests — not vibes.
