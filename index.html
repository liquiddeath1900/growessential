Why us · 02

Methods · 03

No marketing fads.
Just what the soil
responds to.

Every method below is either backed by peer-reviewed research or used at scale by named practitioners. We cite the studies. If the evidence is thin, we say so on the same page we're selling it.

01
Cited recipes

JADAM Korean Natural Farming

Ultra-low-cost microbial inputs you make yourself — JMS, JLF, JWA. Cho Youngsang's open-source farming system. Near-zero cost, built from local leaf mold and household materials.

02
Meta-analysis backed

Vermicompost ("Black Gold")

Worm-castings amendment. Peer-reviewed meta-analysis (Mathieu 2019): +26% commercial yield, +57% root biomass. The strongest-evidenced biological input we sell.

03
Institutional adoption

Johnson-Su Bioreactor

Static no-turn fungal-dominant compost. Dr. David Johnson's protocol. 12-month cycle. Applied as extract, slurry seed coating, or amendment.

04
NRCS-validated

Soil testing + interpretation

Lab chemistry (Logan, Ward, RegenAg) + biological health (Haney, PLFA) + field reads (slake, infiltration, Solvita). Numbers turned into a 90-day plan.

05
Country-screened

Food forest + agroforestry design

Native species + non-invasive companions. Climate-matched, hurricane-resilient. We screen out invasives by country — no neem in DR, no leucaena in pasture, period.

06
Long view

Long-term stewardship

Soil work is multi-year. Retainer engagements track outcomes against baseline tests — not vibes. Quarterly retesting included.
